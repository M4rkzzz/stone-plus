# StonePlus AI Usage Policy

StonePlus is open source under Apache-2.0. Automated coding tools may inspect,
modify, translate, refactor, generate, test, build, and help distribute forks
and derivative works in accordance with `LICENSE`. There is no separate
StonePlus ban on AI-assisted development, training, commercial use, hosted
services, or competing products.

Preserve applicable copyright, license, attribution, and third-party notices.
Clearly describe modifications and do not misrepresent a fork as an official
release. See `NOTICE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, and `TRADEMARKS.md`.

Do not include credentials, account data, or private signing keys in prompts,
logs, generated fixtures, or commits. Follow the user's authorized task and
handle security findings through the process in `SECURITY.md`.

`npm run license:verify` is an offline consistency check usable in any clone.
`npm run identity:verify` verifies official maintainer publishing access; it is
not required for ordinary edits, forks, or redistribution. Historical licensing
is documented in `LICENSE_BOUNDARY.md`.

This guidance adds no conditions to Apache-2.0 or third-party licenses.
